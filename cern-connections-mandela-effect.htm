CERN Connections and the Mandela Effect

In the past, for reasons I’ve kept in confidence, I’ve avoided direct references to CERN.

However, the topic has come up in Theories discussions, and it’s made its way into other threads, as well. So, with that in mind, I’m creating this page for conversations focusing on the specific topics of CERN design, construction, and activity:

  • Dates when tests are run, and if they fit any patterns of Mandela Effect issues — both when alternate or duplicate events happened, as well as when people became aware of their own alternate memories. (For the official timeline of CERN activity, see http://timeline.web.cern.ch/events.)
  • Theories about the construction of CERN, especially its stated and implied purposes.
  • Theories about the physical design of CERN, and odd physical and metaphysical connections. (At the moment, people are talking about similarities with the pineal gland.)

If you have thoughts specific to CERN and potential connections to the Mandela Effect, this is the place to share them.
