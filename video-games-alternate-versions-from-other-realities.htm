Gaming – Alternate Versions or Other Realities?

At this website, people regularly mention video games they recall but those games seem never to have existed. Or they recall scenes or characters that are gone now, with no evidence (in official or unofficial histories) that they ever existed.

This is where to discuss those topics, ask questions, and see if — together — we can find normal explanations (or not).

Note: If you have a personal memory of playing the original, legendary Polybius or seeing it being played (in real time) — not the “Polybius”  game currently available at several download sites —  that kind of discussion is fine.

However, as regards that or similar gaming mysteries & rumors, I will only approve comments if you’ve personally played that or other “never existed” games under discussion, and provide details: when (the year/s )  where (named city/town location or GPS), how you gained access to it, etc.

Effective 7 Jan 2016: In addition, all new comments must include: mainstream links to the game in question; to related, similar games; or to supporting evidence that others recall an alternate version.
